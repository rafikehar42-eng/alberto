LONGINES

Longines is a famous Swiss luxury watch brand known for its elegant designs, precision, craftsmanship, and long tradition of watchmaking.

KEY POINTS

  • Origin: Longines was founded in Switzerland and has a rich heritage in traditional Swiss watchmaking.
  • Headquarters: Saint-Imier, Switzerland.
  • Founded: 1832 by Auguste Agassiz.
  • Famous For: Elegant designs, precision, craftsmanship, reliability, and classic Swiss watchmaking.
  • Popular Collections: HydroConquest, Master Collection, Spirit, Conquest, DolceVita, and Flagship.
  • Special Feature: Longines is known for combining traditional watchmaking heritage with modern technology and precise mechanical movements.
  • Symbol: The Longines winged hourglass logo is one of the oldest registered trademarks still used in its original form.
  • Global Reputation: Longines is recognized worldwide as a prestigious Swiss watch brand with a strong connection to elegance, sports, and precision timekeeping.

Conclusion

Longines represents elegance, precision, heritage, craftsmanship, and timeless style, making it one of the most respected Swiss watch brands in the world.
