THE TECHNOLOGY

ENGINEERED FOR PRECISION

Where Innovation Meets Timeless Craftsmanship.

Discover the sophisticated technologies behind exceptional timepieces, where precision engineering, refined craftsmanship, and modern innovation come together to create enduring performance.

01 / 05

AUTOMATIC MOVEMENT

The Art of Mechanical Precision.

Automatic movement represents the traditional art of mechanical watchmaking, powered by the natural motion of the wearer rather than relying on a conventional battery. Inside the timepiece, precisely engineered gears, springs, and a rotor work together to maintain accurate timekeeping while preserving the craftsmanship and character of fine mechanical watches.

02 / 05

QUARTZ PRECISION

Accuracy Designed for Every Moment.

Quartz precision combines advanced engineering with dependable accuracy. A quartz crystal regulates the movement at a remarkably consistent frequency, allowing the timepiece to deliver reliable performance with minimal maintenance. It is an ideal technology for those who value precision, practicality, and everyday reliability.

03 / 05

ECO-DRIVE TECHNOLOGY

Powered by Light. Designed for Life.

Eco-Drive technology harnesses energy from natural and artificial sources of light and converts it into power for the timepiece. This innovative system reduces the need for frequent battery replacement while delivering dependable performance, making it a refined balance between modern technology, convenience, and sustainable innovation.

04 / 05

CHRONOGRAPH

Precision Timing in Motion.

Chronograph technology combines sophisticated timekeeping with precision timing functionality. Specially engineered pushers and sub-dials allow the wearer to measure elapsed time with accuracy, making the chronograph especially suited to performance-driven lifestyles while adding a distinctive and technical character to the watch.

05 / 05

SMART TECHNOLOGY

Intelligence Meets Modern Timekeeping.

Smart technology brings traditional timekeeping into the modern digital world by combining precise time display with intelligent functionality and connectivity. Depending on the timepiece, these advanced features can support notifications, activity tracking, health and fitness information, and other connected experiences while maintaining a contemporary watch design.

TIMEPIECE CARE

CARE FOR YOUR TIMEPIECE

Preserve Its Precision. Protect Its Character.

Proper care helps preserve the performance, appearance, and longevity of your Alberto timepiece for years to come.

01

KEEP IT CLEAN

Clean your watch regularly with a soft, lint-free cloth to maintain its refined appearance.

02

AVOID EXTREMES

Protect your timepiece from extreme temperatures and conditions that may affect its performance.

03

PROTECT FROM MOISTURE

Avoid unnecessary exposure to water and moisture, especially when your watch is not designed for it.

04

HANDLE WITH CARE

Protect your timepiece from strong impacts, drops, and unnecessary physical stress.

05

STORE PROPERLY

When not in use, keep your watch in a clean, dry, and secure place or watch box.

06

PROFESSIONAL SERVICING

Regular professional inspection and servicing can help maintain your watch's precision and longevity.

07

MAGNETIC FIELDS

Protect the Movement

Keep your timepiece away from strong magnetic fields, which may affect the accuracy and performance of its movement.

08

CROWN & PUSHERS

Secure Before Exposure

Ensure the crown and pushers are properly secured before exposing your watch to water or moisture.

09

STRAPS & BRACELETS

Maintain the Details

Inspect leather straps, metal bracelets, and clasps regularly to maintain comfort, appearance, and secure wear.

10

PROFESSIONAL INSPECTION

Preserve Long-Term Performance

Have your timepiece professionally inspected when needed to maintain its precision, condition, and reliable performance.
